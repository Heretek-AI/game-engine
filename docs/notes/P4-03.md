## 2026-11-02 — CSM math and depth-pass fixes (remediation, PR #193 review)
- `cascade_shadow.cpp`:
  - The box half-extent is now the bounding sphere of the slice around its centroid, plus one texel of snap slack, floored at the nominal density. It is rotation invariant and always holds all 8 slice corners (new check).
  - `texel_world` is the real texel size, 2 * half_extent / 1024, and `ortho_half_extent` agrees with it (new check). Before, snapping used the nominal 4 mm texel while the box held a ~3.5 m slice, so the snap step was not a texel.
  - The center is snapped in absolute light space. Before it was snapped relative to the eye, so it moved with every sub-texel camera nudge.
  - `m[11] = dot(lz_n, center) * inv_d`. Before it used an eye-relative z, which is only correct with the eye at the origin. Points nearer the sun get the smaller depth (new check).
- `csm_depth.vert` now projects through the cascade matrix. The old version drew a full-tile triangle for both proxies and interpolated world positions that did not match the texels, and the pillar proxy covered the whole tile. It now draws the ground quad (6 vertices) and the pillar box (top plus 4 sides, 30 vertices). Faces turned away from the sun collapse, so last-writer-wins is safe without a depth test. The test sets the viewport to the cascade tile.
- Test scene: the sun is now (0.6, -0.4, -1), and the 0.5 m pillar throws a ~1.2% L-shaped shadow. New placement probes check that the ground is dark on the +x and -y (away-from-sun) sides and lit on the sun sides. The stability check now renders frame C (the shifted cascade fit with an unmoved view), so moved screen edges don't count as shimmer: diff 0.0. Shadow-coverage floors were lowered from 2% to 0.5%.
- Goldens regenerated on llvmpipe (Mesa, Ubuntu 24.04). Full local native build plus `engine_cascade_shadows` pass. CSM is still not wired into the production frame (option 1); that is P4-06 or a forward-wire follow-up.
