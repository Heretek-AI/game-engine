## 2026-11-02 — design decision: no engine-shader changes, test-local shade pass
- The checked-in `pbr_shade.frag` blob has no range/spot lanes (light rows are `pos+radius?/color+intensity` pairs with inverse-square falloff) and `slangc` is unavailable, so the P4-01/P4-02 goldens cannot express P4-04 falloff.
- Kept the engine blobs untouched (P4-01 `p4_01_spheres` and P4-02 `p4_02_hdr` goldens unaffected — change is purely additive: new header, new test-local `.frag` + `.inc` mirrors, new CTest target).
- New files:
  - `native/renderer/include/engine/renderer/clustered_lights.hpp` — CPU binner: 16x9x24 grid (3456 clusters), up to 1024 lights, `ClusterRangeWindow` smooth-quadratic falloff, `ClusterSpotTouchesSphere` cone test, `ClusterBinLights` deterministic binning (offset/count cells + packed index list, same record shape as the forward+ tile grid).
  - `native/renderer/shaders/cluster_shade.frag` (+ `cluster_shade_frag_spv.inc` in both `shaders/` and `include/.../shaders/` mirrors, via `embed_spv.py` + glslangValidator `--target-env vulkan1.3`) — fullscreen-triangle shade with cluster lookup, range cutoff + window, spot hard-cut + smoothstep edge, diffuse-only BRDF to keep the golden about lights.
  - `native/tests/test_clustered_lights.cpp` (`engine_clustered_lights` CTest) — CPU contract (21 checks, stub-safe) + GPU golden `p4_04_cluster_320x240`.
- `embed_spv.py` requires the `.spv` input to live inside the repo (cwd confinement); compiled to `native/renderer/shaders/cluster_shade.spv` in-tree then deleted after embedding. Renamed `kCluster_shadeSpv*` → `kClusterShadeFragSpv*` to match the `pbr_shade_frag` naming convention.
- `golden_vk_helper.hpp::GoldenOpenDevice` needs all six PBR blobs even though only shade/post pipelines build — the test includes the vis `.inc`s for the plan `init()`.

## 2026-11-02 — brightness calibration (3 attempts, all recorded)
- Attempt 1: intensity 9000, card radius 200 → all-white frame (each light ≈ albedo/pi·I·window·nDotL ≈ 131 HDR at center; one light alone blows out).
- Attempt 2: intensity 1500/spot 6000, radius 120 → still white (same math, ~20x over).
- Attempt 3 (final): points 0.6, spot 4.0 → card HDR ~0.5, center LDR (214,214,214), hue spread ±15 across the 8x8 grid, corners = sky (26,38,59). All probes pass.
- Lesson: with 64 overlapping windowed lights at z=120px over a 320px frame, per-light intensity must be O(0.5), not O(10³) — inverse-square intuition from the P4-01 single-light setup does not transfer.
