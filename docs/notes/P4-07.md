    reads R/G 2.59 (self-check `pr > 2*pg`).

    differential at floor pixel (100,170): on R/G 1.053, off 1.000,
    delta 0.053 (thresholds 1.02 / 0.03); control requires off < 1.02.
    The differential cancels the direct term, isolating the GI
    contribution independent of driver/exposure; the golden pins
    absolute pixels.
