## 2026-11-01 — Design (why CPU paint, not RmlUi's VK backend)
- Per the §4.13.2 verdict: `RenderInterface_VK` owns its own
  instance/device/swapchain, pins `VK_API_VERSION_1_0`, and bundles its
  own `vulkan.h` + `vk_mem_alloc.h` — all three collide with
  `engine_core`'s volk-loaded Vulkan 1.4 device + engine VMA.
- So: `native/ui` owns a thin `Rml::RenderInterface` (records nothing;
  paint is owned by `GameUi::render()`) + `SystemInterface` (0.0 time)
  and drives `ctx->Update()` at construct/load/click. Pixels come from a
  deterministic CPU rasteriser (background + bar + button + FreeType
  glyph runs from the staged LatoLatin face), so the golden is
  bit-stable on lavapipe and real GPUs alike. The engine-renderer
  composite of these quads is a later slice.

## 2026-11-01 — RmlUi lifetime crashes (3 findings, all in notes)
1. **Close-then-Update re-entrancy**: `load_memory` Closed the old doc
   then `Update()`d before parsing the new one — `EventDispatcher`
   corrupted (SIGSEGV in `DetachAllEvents` via `ReleaseUnloadedDocuments`).
   Probe `/tmp/uispike` showed Close+Update alone survives WITHOUT a
   listener, so the trigger is listener + deferred unload.
2. **Manual `delete` of an attached listener is fatal even after a clean
   `RemoveEventListener`**: bisect showed no-delete survives, delete
   crashes — in BOTH `destroy()`'s Update and `load_memory`'s Update.
   RmlUi 6.3 keeps observer-ptrs (`EnableObserverPtr`) that
   `DetachAllEvents` touches during the deferred unload.
   Fix: the click listener is a **process-lifetime static, never
   deleted** (`UiClickListener()`), rebound to the owning GameUi's
   counter on each `bind_click`; destroy/load just `Close()` and let the
   deferred unload detach it.
3. **Flow layout is unclickable**: the first HUD put the button at
   `(91,1)` (probe: `GetAbsoluteOffset`) while the paint put it at
   `(470,10)` — clicks at the painted rect missed. Fix: the HUD uses
   **absolute positioning** in the 1280x720 canvas
   (`#bar` full-width 112px, `#lbl` at (32,28), `#btn` at
   (940,20) 308x72); the CPU paint uses the same rects at 0.5x.
   Probe with absolute layout: `btn abs=(940,20)`, injected click at
   (1094,56) fires exactly once, background click fires zero.
